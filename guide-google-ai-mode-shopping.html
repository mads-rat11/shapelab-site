How to get your products into Google AI Mode shopping

By the AISLO team. Updated 25 November 2026.

Short answer

Google AI Mode shopping combines Gemini with the Shopping Graph, Google's product dataset of more than 50 billion listings. Products reach it through Google Merchant Center. To show up, keep your Merchant Center feed complete and accurate, fill every product attribute, match it with on-page structured data and fix disapprovals quickly.

Key points

  • AI Mode shopping is powered by Gemini plus the Shopping Graph.
  • Google says about 2 billion listings are refreshed every hour.
  • Your Merchant Center feed is the main way products reach AI Mode.
  • Merchant Center is adding AI performance insights and an attribute completeness score.

How does Google AI Mode choose products?

Google describes AI Mode shopping as Gemini working with the Shopping Graph. The Shopping Graph holds more than 50 billion product listings, with around 2 billion refreshed every hour, each with details such as reviews, prices, colour options and availability. When a shopper asks a question, AI Mode builds a panel of products that match the request.

Those listings come from Google Merchant Center. In May 2026 Google announced AI performance insights in Merchant Center to show how products are discovered in AI Mode, AI Overviews and the Gemini app, along with an attribute completeness score that flags products missing structured attributes.

How do I get my products into AI Mode shopping?

  1. Connect a clean Merchant Center feed. Fix every disapproval and warning first; a disapproved product cannot be shown.
  2. Fill every attribute. GTIN, brand, size, colour, material, age group and product category. Use the attribute completeness score as your to-do list.
  3. Write descriptive titles. Lead with what the product is, then the attributes people ask about.
  4. Match your site to your feed. On-page Product structured data should agree with the feed on price and availability.
  5. Collect product reviews. Reviews are part of the product details shown in the Shopping Graph.
  6. Keep images clean. Clear product images on plain backgrounds, plus lifestyle images where the category needs them.
  7. Turn on agentic checkout where available. Google is rolling out agentic checkout in the US. Shopify merchants can manage direct checkout for AI Mode in Agentic Storefronts.

Is AI Mode different from Google Shopping ads?

Yes. Ads are paid placements. AI Mode product panels draw on the Shopping Graph, which includes free listings from Merchant Center. Good feed data helps both.

Questions

Do I need Google Merchant Center for AI Mode?

Merchant Center is how most stores get products into the Shopping Graph that powers AI Mode shopping. Shopify syncs product data to Merchant Center through its Google channel.

What is the attribute completeness score?

A Merchant Center measure Google announced in May 2026 that shows which products are missing structured product attributes.

Does structured data on my site matter if I have a feed?

Yes. Keep on-page Product markup consistent with your feed so price and availability agree everywhere Google reads them.

Is AI Mode available everywhere?

Features roll out by market. Google first launched several AI Mode shopping features, including agentic checkout, in the US.
