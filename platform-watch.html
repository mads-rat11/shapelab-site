Questions

What does AISLO Platform watch track?

Policy updates, feed specification changes, new attributes, crawler changes, new checkout and catalog programmes, and announcements from more than 10 AI shopping platforms, including Google Merchant Center, Google AI Mode, Gemini, ChatGPT, Shopify Catalog, Microsoft, Meta, Perplexity, Amazon and TikTok Shop.

How does AISLO stay up to date?

AISLO reads platform documentation, changelogs, help centres and announcements every day. When something changes, it updates its checks and recommendations automatically, so your next scan already reflects the new rules.

Will I be told when a change affects my store?

Yes. Each change is matched against your feeds, products and site files. You get an alert only when it affects you, with the fix ready to approve.
