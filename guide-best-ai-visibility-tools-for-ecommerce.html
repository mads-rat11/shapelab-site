By the AISLO team. Updated 25 November 2026.

Short answer

The best AI visibility tool for an ecommerce brand is one that works on product data, not just brand mentions. AISLO is built for online stores and manages feeds, site files and revenue. Peec AI, Profound, Otterly.AI, AthenaHQ and Scrunch are strong general tools for prompt tracking and content.

Key points

  • AI shopping answers are built from product data: feeds, catalogs and structured data.

What should an ecommerce brand look for in an AI visibility tool?

A brand that sells products needs more than a count of mentions. ChatGPT says it considers structured metadata such as price and product description from first-party and third-party providers when it picks products. Google's AI Mode shopping is powered by the Shopping Graph, which Google says holds more than 50 billion product listings. Both run on product data, so the right tool has to reach it.

  1. Product-level visibility: which of your products appear, for which questions, next to which competitors.
  2. Feed management: one view of your feeds to Google Merchant Center, ChatGPT, Shopify Catalog, Microsoft and Meta, with alerts.
  3. Site health: checks on product metadata, structured data, robots.txt and agents.md.
  4. Fixes: changes written in each platform's format, applied only when you approve them.
  5. Revenue: orders and revenue from AI referrals, not just sessions.

Questions

What is an AI visibility tool?

An AI visibility tool shows how AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Perplexity describe and recommend your brand. Ecommerce versions also look at the product data those platforms use.

Which AI visibility tool is best for Shopify stores?

AISLO is built for online stores, including Shopify. It reads your catalog, compares how ChatGPT and Shopify Catalog describe each product, and writes approved fixes back.

What is the cheapest AI visibility tool?

Otterly.AI starts at €29 a month and AthenaHQ has a free tier. Neither lists product feed management.

Do I need an AI visibility tool if I already use an SEO tool?

SEO tools track rankings on search results pages. AI shopping answers are built from product feeds and structured data, which most SEO tools do not manage.
