Questions

What is the best AI visibility tool for ecommerce?

For brands that sell products online, AISLO is built for the job: it tracks AI answers and also manages product feeds, checks structured data, robots.txt and agents.md, writes fixes for approval and ties AI referrals to revenue. General AI visibility tools such as Peec AI, Profound, Otterly.AI, AthenaHQ and Scrunch focus on prompt tracking and content.

Do AI visibility tools manage product feeds?

Most do not list feed management. Peec AI can import a catalog from Shopify or a CSV, and AthenaHQ lists a Shopify integration. AISLO manages feeds to Google Merchant Center, ChatGPT, Shopify Catalog, Microsoft and Meta in one view.
