AISLO vs Peec AI

Short answer

Peec AI is a well-priced AI search analytics tool from Berlin, popular with marketing teams and agencies. Peec AI tells you how often you show up. AISLO also manages the feeds and files that decide whether you show up, and shows what it earns. If you sell products online, AISLO covers the work that decides whether AI recommends them.

Why ecommerce teams choose AISLO

Feeds, not just mentions

AI shopping ranks products from feed and catalog data. AISLO manages your feeds to Google Merchant Center, ChatGPT, Shopify Catalog, Microsoft and Meta in one place, and alerts you when one breaks.

The hidden layer, checked

Product metadata, structured data, robots.txt and agents.md are checked on every scan, so a plugin update or a missing GTIN does not quietly hide your products.

Revenue, not vanity metrics

AISLO matches AI referral sessions to orders, so you can see which fixes paid for themselves, product by product.

When Peec AI may suit you better

Choose Peec AI if you mainly need prompt tracking across several countries and models for a marketing team, and someone else already owns your feeds and site files.
