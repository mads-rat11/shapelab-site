Trust Center

Your data, your approval, your call

AISLO works with your store and your Google accounts. Here is exactly what we access, what we do with it, and how you stay in control.

Read-only analytics

Search Console and Google Analytics are connected with read-only access. We use them to see how shoppers reach you from AI platforms. We never write to them.

Approval before changes

Where AISLO can update your Merchant Center, store catalog or feeds, every change is shown to you first. Nothing is sent until you approve it, and each approval is logged.

Limited use of Google data

Data from Google APIs is used only to provide AISLO's features to you. It is never sold, never used for advertising, and never used to train general AI models. See our Privacy Policy.

Deletion on request

Disconnect any account at any time from your settings or from the provider's own security page. Ask us and we delete the data we hold from that account.

How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit with TLS and at rest. Access tokens for your connected accounts are stored encrypted and used only by the services that need them. Staff access to customer data is limited to the people who need it to support you.

Found a security issue or have a question about how we handle data? Email hello@useaislo.com with "Security" in the subject line and we will respond promptly.
