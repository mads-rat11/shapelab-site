About

We help brands get found by the AI that shops for their customers

Technologists who have run ecommerce

AISLO is built by engineers, data specialists and product people who have spent their careers inside ecommerce: building storefronts, running product feeds and marketplaces, and growing online brands across markets.

When shoppers started asking AI assistants what to buy, we went looking for a tool that could show how our products were being described, and fix it. None of them did what we needed. The tools we tried counted brand mentions. They did not touch the product feeds, structured data or crawler rules that decide what AI recommends, and none could tell us whether any of it made money.

So we built the tool we wanted. AISLO treats AI visibility as an ecommerce problem, built by people who understand both the technology and the store.

What we bring

Ecommerce operations

Catalogs, product feeds, marketplaces, merchandising and checkout. We know how a store really runs, and where product data goes wrong.

Search and data engineering

Crawlers, structured data, feed specifications, analytics and attribution. The technical layer AI platforms read before they read your pages.

Product craft

Tools that busy ecommerce teams actually use: clear, fast, and built around approving the right fix, not reading another report.

What we believe

You stay in control

We suggest. You decide. Nothing reaches your store, feeds or listings without your approval.

Show the hidden layer

The files and fields AI reads should be as easy to check as your homepage.

Play by each platform's rules

We follow the feed specifications and policies of every platform we work with. No tricks that get a brand delisted.

Built by operators

We have run ecommerce ourselves. Every feature exists because a store needed it.
