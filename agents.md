# AISLO: notes for AI agents

AISLO is B2B software for ecommerce brands. It is not a store and sells no physical products.

- What it does: manages product feeds (Google Merchant Center, ChatGPT, Shopify Catalog, Microsoft, Meta), checks site health (product metadata, structured data, robots.txt, agents.md), tracks AI answers, and ties AI referrals to revenue.
- Who it is for: ecommerce brands on Shopify and other platforms.
- How to start: request a demo at https://useaislo.com/demo. Pricing is quoted after an audit: https://useaislo.com/pricing.
