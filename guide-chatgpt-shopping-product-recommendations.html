How to get your products recommended in ChatGPT shopping

By the AISLO team. Updated 25 November 2026.

Short answer

ChatGPT picks products itself; they are not ads. It weighs your query against product data such as price and description from merchants and third parties. To be recommended, let OpenAI's search crawler in, publish complete structured data, send a product feed (directly or through Shopify Catalog) and write titles that match how shoppers ask.

Key points

  • Product results in ChatGPT are selected by ChatGPT and are not ads.
  • ChatGPT uses structured metadata like price and description from first-party and third-party sources.
  • Merchants can apply to send OpenAI a direct product feed.
  • Eligible Shopify stores are discoverable in ChatGPT through Shopify Catalog.

How does ChatGPT choose which products to show?

OpenAI says product results are selected independently by ChatGPT, are not ads and are not influenced by OpenAI partnerships. A product appears when ChatGPT judges it relevant to the shopper's intent, using the question plus context such as Memory. When deciding, it considers structured metadata from first-party and third-party providers, for example price and product description, along with other third-party content.

ChatGPT can also show review summaries generated from reviews on public websites, and labels such as "Budget-friendly". For some eligible products and merchants it offers Instant Checkout inside ChatGPT.

How do I get my products recommended in ChatGPT?

  1. Let OpenAI's search crawler in. Check that robots.txt does not block OAI-SearchBot. See our robots.txt guide.
  2. Publish complete product structured data. Price, availability, brand, GTIN, images and reviews in Product markup on every product page.
  3. Send a product feed. OpenAI invites merchants to apply for a direct product feed so ChatGPT reflects current product information. On Shopify, eligible stores are discoverable through Shopify Catalog by default.
  4. Write titles and attributes the way shoppers ask. "Copper peptide serum, 30 ml, fragrance free" matches more questions than "Serum".
  5. Earn reviews on public sites. ChatGPT's review summaries come from public websites, so reviews that only live behind a login do not help.
  6. Keep price and stock in sync. A feed that disagrees with your site gives ChatGPT a reason to pick another merchant.
  7. Track the questions that matter. Run your category's real questions regularly and record which products appear, in what position, against which competitors.

How do I measure sales from ChatGPT?

Shopify says orders that come through its ChatGPT integration appear in the Shopify admin with ChatGPT referral attribution. For other stacks, tag ChatGPT referral sessions in your analytics and match them to orders. AISLO does this for you by platform and product.

Questions

Can I pay to appear in ChatGPT shopping results?

No. OpenAI says product results are selected independently and are not ads. Ads in ChatGPT are shown separately.

How do I submit products to ChatGPT?

OpenAI lets merchants apply to provide a direct product feed. Eligible Shopify merchants are discoverable through Shopify Catalog without a separate integration.

Does robots.txt affect ChatGPT shopping?

It can. If OAI-SearchBot is blocked, ChatGPT search cannot read your pages directly. Check the file after every plugin or theme change.

How long does it take to show up?

There is no published timeline. Fixing feed errors and structured data is the fastest lever because it changes the data ChatGPT already reads.
