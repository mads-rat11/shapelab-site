AI shopping glossary

The terms that come up when you work on how AI assistants find and recommend your products, defined in one sentence each.

AI visibility
How often, and how accurately, AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Perplexity mention and recommend a brand or its products.
Generative engine optimisation (GEO)
The practice of improving how content and products appear in answers written by AI assistants, as opposed to ranked links on a search results page.
AI shopping
Product discovery and buying inside AI assistants, such as ChatGPT shopping results, Google AI Mode product panels and Shopify Agentic Storefronts.
Product feed
A structured file or API stream of a store's products, with fields such as ID, title, price, availability, brand and GTIN, sent to platforms like Google Merchant Center or ChatGPT.
Feed destination
A platform that receives a product feed, for example Google Merchant Center, ChatGPT, Shopify Catalog, Microsoft Merchant Center or Meta catalog.
Google Merchant Center
Google's tool for submitting product data. Its listings feed the Shopping Graph that powers Google AI Mode shopping, AI Overviews and Gemini.
Shopping Graph
Google's product dataset, with more than 50 billion listings, used by AI Mode and Google Shopping to answer shopping questions.
Shopify Catalog
Shopify's structured product data system that makes eligible merchants' products discoverable in AI channels such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot and Google AI Mode through Agentic Storefronts.
Agentic checkout
A purchase completed by an AI assistant on the shopper's behalf, after the shopper confirms, without leaving the conversation.
Structured data
Machine-readable markup, usually schema.org Product markup, that tells crawlers a product's price, availability, brand, identifiers and reviews.
GTIN
Global Trade Item Number: the barcode identifier platforms use to match a product to reviews, comparisons and other sellers.
robots.txt
A file at the root of a website that tells crawlers, including AI crawlers such as OAI-SearchBot and PerplexityBot, which pages they may read.
agents.md
A plain-language file for AI agents that visit a site, describing what the business sells, where to find it and how to buy.
llms.txt
A markdown file at the root of a website that gives AI models a short guide to the site's most useful pages.
Share of AI answers
The percentage of tracked AI answers for a set of questions that mention or recommend a brand.
SKU
Stock keeping unit: one sellable item, including each variant that has its own identifier. AISLO is priced by the number of SKUs it manages.
