What is AISLO?

AISLO is AI visibility software for ecommerce brands. It manages your product feeds in one place, checks the files AI crawlers read on your site, tracks how ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Shopify Catalog recommend your products, and writes fixes for you to approve.

Feeds and site health

Every feed. Every file. One place.

You send product feeds to Google, ChatGPT, Shopify, Microsoft and Meta. Each has its own format, its own rules and its own errors, and keeping track of all of them is hard.

Most of what AI platforms read about your store sits behind your website, where nobody looks. AISLO brings all of it into one view, so you can see what is happening and fix it.

The part of your store AI reads first, and you see last

Product metadata

Titles, identifiers, sizes, materials and stock. The fields AI platforms match against when a shopper asks for something specific.

Structured data

The markup on your product pages that tells crawlers the price, rating and availability. When it is missing or wrong, the answer is too.

robots.txt

Which AI crawlers you let in and which you shut out. One line added by a plugin or a developer can hide your store without anyone noticing.

agents.md

Plain instructions for AI agents that visit your site: what you sell, where to find it, and how to buy. We check whether you have one and what it says.

Questions

What is AISLO?

AISLO is AI visibility software for ecommerce brands. It manages your product feeds in one place, checks the files AI crawlers read on your site, tracks how ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and Shopify Catalog recommend your products, writes fixes for you to approve, and shows the revenue that follows.

Who is AISLO for?

Ecommerce brands and retailers that sell online, on Shopify or any other platform, and want their products recommended when shoppers ask AI assistants what to buy.

How is AISLO different from other AI visibility tools?

Most AI visibility tools track how often a brand is mentioned. AISLO works on the product data AI shopping is built from: feeds, product attributes, structured data and crawler access, and it measures orders, not just mentions.

Which AI platforms does AISLO cover?

AISLO tracks answers in ChatGPT, Google AI Mode and AI Overviews, and Shopify Catalog, manages feeds to Google Merchant Center, ChatGPT, Shopify Catalog, Microsoft and Meta, and watches 14 AI shopping platforms for changes.

How much does AISLO cost?

AISLO is quoted on the number of SKUs you manage, the integrations you need and the support you want. Every engagement starts with a free visibility audit.
