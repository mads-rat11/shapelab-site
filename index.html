FAQ

Questions operators ask.

Is this a dashboard or an agent?

An agent. The dashboard on the roadmap is how you read and steer it, but the work happens on a schedule whether you open it or not.

Which platforms does it work with?

Shopify Admin GraphQL, Klaviyo, Amplitude, Google Merchant Center, Amazon Seller Central, Meta Ads, Microsoft Ads and Peec AI today. Each is wrapped as an MCP server, so new systems are added the same way.

Will it publish things I have not approved?

Outbound messages pass a shared tone specification and a pre-send claim check. You decide which pipelines write automatically and which wait for approval.

Why does the claim check matter?

Cosmetic claims are regulated: MoCRA and FDA labelling rules in the US, CPNP in the EU. One wrong word in a reply or an email is a compliance problem, so every claim is checked before anything goes out.

Do I need to be at a computer?

No. The agents run in the cloud on their own schedules. You approve, preview and redirect them from your phone.

Who is it for?

DTC operators with real operational complexity, such as inventory, several markets and regulated products, and not enough people to run a growth team.

When can I use it?

The production pipelines run on koppa beauty now. We are onboarding a small group of operators ahead of the spend, prediction and reorder agents. Request access above.
