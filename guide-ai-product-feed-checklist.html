The AI product feed checklist for online stores

By the AISLO team. Updated 25 November 2026.

Short answer

AI shopping platforms build answers from your product feed, so every product needs a unique ID, a descriptive title, a full description, price, availability, brand, GTIN, clear images, a product link and its key attributes such as size, colour and material. Then keep feeds in sync with your site and check them weekly.

Key points

  • Feeds now supply AI assistants as well as search engines.
  • The same core attributes serve Merchant Center, ChatGPT and Shopify Catalog.
  • Price and stock must agree between your feed and your site.
  • Check every destination weekly; errors often appear without warning.

Which attributes does every product need?

  • Unique IDStable across every feed and never reused.
  • Descriptive titleWhat it is, then brand, size, colour or material. Not just a product name.
  • Full descriptionPlain facts a shopper would ask about: use, ingredients or materials, dimensions, care.
  • Price and availabilityMatching your site exactly, including sale prices and dates.
  • Brand and GTINIdentifiers are how platforms match your product to reviews and comparisons.
  • ImagesA clear main image, plus extra angles. No text overlays on the main image.
  • Product linkTo a live, crawlable page with matching structured data.
  • Variant attributesSize, colour, material and pattern on each variant, grouped with an item group ID.
  • CategoryThe platform's product category, not just your own collection name.
  • Shipping and returnsCosts, times and return window, so assistants can answer delivery questions.

What should I check every week?

  1. Disapproved and warned products in each destination.
  2. Price and stock mismatches between feed and site.
  3. New products missing GTIN or key attributes.
  4. Changes to robots.txt or structured data after site updates.
  5. Which products AI assistants actually recommend, and for which questions.

AISLO runs these checks across all five destinations and alerts you when something breaks. See feeds and site health.

Questions

Is one feed enough for every AI platform?

The core attributes overlap, but each destination has its own format, rules and errors. Build from one clean source of product data, then check each destination separately.

What is the most common feed error?

Mismatched price or availability between the feed and the product page, followed by missing identifiers such as GTIN.

Do Shopify stores need a separate ChatGPT feed?

Eligible Shopify stores are discoverable in ChatGPT through Shopify Catalog. Non-Shopify merchants can apply to OpenAI for a direct product feed.

How often should feeds update?

As often as price and stock change. Daily is a minimum for most stores; fast-moving stock needs more frequent updates.
