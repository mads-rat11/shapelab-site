Who we are

AISLO is operated by AISLO Technologies Ltd, a company registered in the United Kingdom ("AISLO", "we", "us"). This policy explains how we handle personal data when you visit useaislo.com, contact us, or use the AISLO service.

What we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, work email, company, store website, and anything you write in our forms or emails.
  • Account data: when you use AISLO, the login details and settings for your account.
  • Connected account data: when you connect a store or a Google account, the product, feed, search and analytics data that connection allows, as described on our Trust Center.
  • Website data: basic technical data such as browser type and pages visited, collected by our hosting provider to run and secure the site.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and arrange demos and audits.
  • To provide, maintain and support the AISLO service you signed up for.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent misuse.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use connected account data for advertising.

Google user data

AISLO's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

  • We use data from Google Merchant Center, Search Console and Google Analytics only to provide and improve the user-facing features of AISLO that you request.
  • We do not transfer this data to others except as needed to provide those features, to comply with law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you.
  • We do not use this data for serving advertisements, and we do not sell it.
  • We do not use this data to train generalised AI or machine learning models.
  • People at AISLO do not read this data unless you ask us to for support, it is needed for security, or the law requires it.

You can revoke AISLO's access at any time from your Google Account's security settings.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers that help us run AISLO, such as hosting, email and form delivery, under contracts that limit their use of it. We may also disclose data when the law requires it.

Retention

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and follow up. We keep account and connected data while your account is active, and delete it within a reasonable period after you close your account or ask us to.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. To make a request, email us. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. If you are in the UK and unhappy with how we handle your data, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Contact

AISLO Technologies Ltd, United Kingdom. Email hello@useaislo.com.

We may update this policy. When we make material changes we will update the date above and, where appropriate, tell you directly.
