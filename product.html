In short

AISLO does four jobs for an online store: it measures how AI assistants recommend your products, manages your product feeds in one place, writes fixes to feeds, listings and site files for you to approve, and ties AI referrals to revenue.

Product metadata

Titles, identifiers, sizes, materials and stock. The fields AI platforms match against when a shopper asks for something specific.

Structured data

The markup on your product pages that tells crawlers the price, rating and availability. When it is missing or wrong, the answer is too.

robots.txt

Which AI crawlers you let in and which you shut out. One line added by a plugin or a developer can hide your store without anyone noticing.

agents.md

Plain instructions for AI agents that visit your site: what you sell, where to find it, and how to buy. We check whether you have one and what it says.

Built for how you sell

For Shopify brands

Shopify already shares your catalog with AI platforms. AISLO shows you how that catalog reads in ChatGPT and Shopify's own agents, finds the products that are missing or misdescribed, and fixes them at the source.

  • Catalog and storefront compared product by product
  • Fixes written back to Shopify after approval
  • Google Merchant Center kept in step

For every other stack

On WooCommerce, BigCommerce, Adobe Commerce or a custom build, there is no single catalog doing the work for you. AISLO builds the feeds each platform asks for from your product data, checks them, and delivers them once you approve.

  • Validation before anything is sent
  • Scheduled refresh so prices and stock stay current
