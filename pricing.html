Questions

How is AISLO priced?

AISLO is priced on three things: the number of SKUs you manage, the integrations you need, and the level of support you want. We send a tailored quote after a free visibility audit of your catalog.

Why do you price by SKU?

The work AISLO does grows with your catalog: every SKU is tracked in AI answers, checked in each feed and kept in sync with your site. Pricing by SKU means you pay for the catalog you actually run, not for seats or features you do not use.

What counts as an integration?

Each place AISLO reads from or sends to: feed destinations such as Google Merchant Center, ChatGPT and Meta, your store platform or PIM, and measurement tools such as Google Analytics. Most brands start with three to five.

Is there a free trial?

Every engagement starts with a free visibility audit. We scan your top products across ChatGPT, Google AI and Shopify Catalog, check your feeds and site files, and walk you through the results. You keep the report.

Do you charge per user?

No. Every plan includes unlimited team seats, so marketing, ecommerce and development can all work in AISLO.

How long are agreements?

Agreements are usually annual. Ask us about shorter terms for smaller catalogs.

Will AISLO change my store or listings?

Only with your approval. Every fix is shown to you first, and you can approve, edit or skip it. Search Console and Analytics are connected read only.
