Questions

How much does AISLO cost?

Starter is $199 a month and Growth is $499 a month, or $165 and $415 a month when billed yearly. Scale is priced for your brands and regions. Every plan starts with a free visibility audit.

What is included in the free audit?

We scan your top products across ChatGPT, Google AI and Shopify Catalog, check your feeds and the files behind your site, and walk you through the results on a call. You keep the report, whether or not you sign up.

What counts as a feed destination?

Each place your product data is sent: Google Merchant Center, ChatGPT, Shopify Catalog, Microsoft Merchant Center and Meta catalog.

Do I need to be on Shopify?

No. Shopify brands get a direct catalog connection. On any other platform we build and deliver your feeds from your product data.

Will AISLO change my store or listings?

Only with your approval. Every fix is shown to you first, and you can approve, edit or skip it. Search Console and Analytics are connected read only.

Can I change plans or cancel?

Yes. Monthly plans can be upgraded, downgraded or cancelled at any time. Yearly plans renew once a year. When you leave you can disconnect every account and ask us to delete your data.

Do prices include tax?

Prices are in US dollars and exclude VAT and sales tax, which are added where they apply.
