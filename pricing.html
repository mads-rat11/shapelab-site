Questions

How much does AISLO cost?

We are onboarding brands in small groups during early access. Pricing depends on how many products and feeds you have, so we quote after your audit. There is no long contract: plans run month to month.

What is included in the visibility audit?

We scan your top products across ChatGPT, Google AI and Shopify Catalog, check your feeds and the files behind your site, and walk you through the results on a call. You keep the report.

Do I need to be on Shopify?

No. Shopify brands get a direct catalog connection. On any other platform we build and deliver your feeds from your product data.

Will AISLO change my store or listings?

Only with your approval. Every fix is shown to you first, and you can approve, edit or skip it. Search Console and Analytics are connected read only.

How long does setup take?

Connecting accounts takes a few minutes. Your first scan is ready once we have read your catalog and feeds.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Plans run month to month. When you leave you can disconnect every account and ask us to delete your data.
