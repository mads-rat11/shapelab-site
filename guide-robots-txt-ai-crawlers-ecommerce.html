robots.txt for AI crawlers: what an online store should allow

By the AISLO team. Updated 25 November 2026.

Short answer

An online store that wants to appear in AI shopping answers should allow the search crawlers AI assistants use to read pages: Googlebot, Bingbot, OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot and Claude-SearchBot. Training crawlers such as GPTBot, Google-Extended and ClaudeBot are a separate choice. Check the file after every plugin, theme or CDN change.

Key points

  • Search crawlers and training crawlers are different bots with different names.
  • Blocking a search crawler can remove your pages from that assistant's answers.
  • Google-Extended does not control whether you appear in Google Search.
  • robots.txt is a request, and one line from a plugin can change it.

What does a good robots.txt look like for an online store?

This example lets every search and assistant crawler in, keeps private areas closed, and points to the sitemap. Change the training bots to Disallow: / if you do not want your content used for model training.

User-agent: *
Disallow: /cart
Disallow: /checkout
Disallow: /account

User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
Allow: /

User-agent: ChatGPT-User
Allow: /

User-agent: PerplexityBot
Allow: /

User-agent: Claude-SearchBot
Allow: /

User-agent: GPTBot
Allow: /

User-agent: Google-Extended
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://www.example.com/sitemap.xml

What are the most common robots.txt mistakes?

  1. A security or SEO plugin blocks AI bots by default. Check the live file after installing or updating one.
  2. A CDN or firewall blocks the bot before robots.txt is read. Bot protection settings can override an Allow line.
  3. A staging rule ships to production. Disallow: / under User-agent: * hides the whole store.
  4. Product pages blocked by a parameter rule. Over-broad rules for filters can catch product URLs too.

Questions

Should I block GPTBot?

GPTBot is OpenAI's training crawler. Blocking it is a choice about model training; it is separate from OAI-SearchBot, which ChatGPT uses for search results.

Does blocking Google-Extended remove me from AI Overviews?

No. Google says Google-Extended does not affect inclusion in Google Search. AI Overviews and AI Mode use Googlebot.

Is robots.txt enough to control AI crawlers?

No. robots.txt is a request that reputable crawlers follow. Firewalls and CDN bot settings can also block or allow bots.

How often should I check robots.txt?

After every plugin, theme, CDN or platform change, and at least weekly. AISLO checks it on every scan.
